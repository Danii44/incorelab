OCTO SMM — Social Media Marketing Studio (Demo Website)
=========================================================

In the style of the Octo SMM screenshots you sent: black background,
vibrant pink #F70670, off-white #F1F9F4, Onest headings and Manrope body
text, big stacked "octo smm studio" hero, and the line "We turn
subscribers into clients."

HOW TO OPEN (no setup needed):
  1. Unzip this folder
  2. Open index.html in any browser (double-click it)
  3. That's it — no server, no install, no build step
  (Keep the images/ folder next to index.html, or the octopus PNGs
  won't load. The Google Fonts and Unsplash photos need internet.)

WHAT'S INSIDE:
  - index.html : the whole site (HTML + CSS + small JavaScript)
  - images/hero-octopus.png : original pink octopus mascot
  - images/octopus-tentacles.png : original pink tentacles creative
  - Hero with sample stats, next-gen SMM intro, impact stats,
    6 services, creatives gallery, 3 sample case studies,
    4 sample team members, Typography & Colors section
    (Onest / Manrope, 000000 / F1F9F4 / F70670), contact form

SAMPLE CONTENT:
  All stats ($60k+, 1.3k+, $613k, 3x, +85%), cases, team names and
  client results are samples. The octopus images are original AI
  images made for this demo — not the original artwork from the
  screenshots. Replace names, numbers and photos with your real
  studio details before using it for a real business.

  The contact form is a front-end demo only — it shows a thank-you
  message and does not send email anywhere.

EDITING:
  Open index.html in any text editor, change the text, numbers and
  image paths, save, and refresh the browser.
